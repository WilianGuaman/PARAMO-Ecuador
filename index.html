Methodology, provenance & citation

Understand what is a model result, what is reference-system data and how uncertainty is represented.

Research documentation

PARAMO public results layer

The dashboard displays precomputed results generated with PARAMO. The optimization formulation, GAMS implementation, private InputData workbooks, GDX/LST files and solver logs are not distributed.

Study cases

Ecuador 24-Bus is the national planning application with REF/BRIDGE/NZT pathways and W100/W5 hydrological ensembles. Ecuador 6-Bus is a reduced planning case with BAU/REN100 under Normal/Extreme hydrology.

Uncertainty

Baseline national results contain 100 realizations; Adverse national results contain five. Ensemble statistics are therefore labelled explicitly and should not be interpreted as having identical statistical depth.

Geography

24-bus map coordinates and canonical planning zones are taken from the Ecuador Power DataHub crosswalk. Colombia and Peru are shown as external systems using clearly identified model/display anchors. Reference plants remain contextual assets rather than one-to-one optimization objects.

Foundational publication

Guamán, W., Benalcazar, P., Cordova-Garcia, J., & Torres, M. (2025). An integrated framework for the optimal expansion of hydro-dependent power systems under water-resource uncertainty. Energy Conversion and Management: X, 28, 101297. DOI: 10.1016/j.ecmx.2025.101297.

Study design

The national model compares REF, BRIDGE and NZT over 2025–2050. Baseline contains 100 realizations per pathway and Adverse contains five. Detailed profiles use REF–w23, BRIDGE–w17 and NZT–w22 in Baseline, and REF–w5, BRIDGE–w3 and NZT–w3 in Adverse.

Representative realizations are selected using total cost: proximity to the mean within the modal interval for Baseline, and proximity to the median for Adverse. Means and empirical intervals describe the ensemble; the selected realization is one individual plan.

Each model year contains twelve 720-hour periods. Seasonal calendars and operating assumptions are specific to each hydrological case. ENS is reconstructed from nodal power deficits and monthly duration.

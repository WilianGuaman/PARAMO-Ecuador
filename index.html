Research documentation

PARAMO public results layer

The dashboard displays precomputed outputs generated with PARAMO. The GAMS implementation, InputData workbooks, GDX/LST/log files and full generator-level result tables are not distributed.

Ecuador 6-Bus

The reduced case contains four validated W=1 configurations: BAU and REN100 under Normal and Extreme hydrology. The public layer reports system aggregates, corridor decisions and selected hydraulic cascades.

Ecuador 24-Bus

The national planning application contains REF, BRIDGE and NZT pathways. Baseline uses W100 and Adverse uses W5. The browser exposes aggregate statistics and representative trajectories, not realization-level tables.

Geography

Model nodes and reduced corridors are planning abstractions. Reference plants and the georeferenced 24-bus network provide physical context and are visually distinguished from optimization objects. Colombia and Peru remain external systems.

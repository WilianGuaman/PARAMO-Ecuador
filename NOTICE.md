# Notice

**PARAMO** means **Planning And Resource Allocation under Multi-scenario Optimization**.

PARAMO Ecuador Results Explorer, its original public presentation, PARAMO-derived visualization layer, figures and documentation are curated by **Wilian Guamán Cuenca**.

The public repository is a visualization and documentation release. The PARAMO optimization engine, private model databases, complete result workbooks and non-public model assets are not distributed here.

The public map contains:

- a mainland Ecuador outline derived from Natural Earth public-domain geography;
- the existing 230 and 500 kV subset of the user-supplied `Lineas.geojson` layer;
- PARAMO 24-node and 6-node reduced planning networks;
- contextual reference plants and international anchors.

The georeferenced electricity-line source files did not include a complete external citation or license statement. Attribution and redistribution remain subject to the authoritative source terms retained by the repository owner. Lower-voltage features are intentionally excluded from the public v2.5.0 map.

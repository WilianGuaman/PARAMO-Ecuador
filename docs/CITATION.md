# Citation

## PARAMO Ecuador Results Explorer

When using the interactive explorer, public result tables, figures or derived data, cite the repository:

> Guamán Cuenca, W. (2026). *PARAMO Ecuador Results Explorer: Planning And Resource Allocation under Multi-scenario Optimization* (Version 1.1.0) [Software and public research results]. GitHub. https://github.com/WilianGuaman/PARAMO-Ecuador

Machine-readable formats are available in the repository root:

- `CITATION.cff`
- `docs/citation/CITATION.bib`
- `docs/citation/CITATION.ris`

## Foundational methodology

For the integrated generation/transmission expansion, reconductoring, reservoir-operation and hydrological-uncertainty methodology, cite:

> Guamán, W., Benalcazar, P., Cordova-Garcia, J., & Torres, M. (2025). *An integrated framework for the optimal expansion of hydro-dependent power systems under water-resource uncertainty*. **Energy Conversion and Management: X, 28**, 101297. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecmx.2025.101297

The explorer contains PARAMO planning results in addition to the methodological foundation described in the article. Reuse of the public results should identify the explorer or archived dataset as the data source, and the scholarly publication where methodological attribution is relevant.

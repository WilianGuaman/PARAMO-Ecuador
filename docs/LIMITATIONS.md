# Interpretation boundaries

- The Baseline national ensemble contains 100 realizations and the Adverse ensemble contains five; their statistical depth is not equivalent.
- Detailed national annual/monthly operational trajectories use representative realizations where ensemble-wide time-series exports are not part of the public dataset.
- The 24-bus public dataset reports transmission investment at aggregate level. Corridor-level optimized line-build/reconductoring decisions and line-flow variables are outside the public distribution.
- The 6-bus case is a reduced planning representation and is not a one-to-one replacement for the national 24-bus representation.
- Cross-case comparisons are restricted to compatible metrics and should be interpreted in the context of the different system representations.

## Geographic interpretation

External-system markers for Colombia and Peru are included to make the planning topology explicit. Where a verified physical counterpart coordinate is not part of the public crosswalk, the marker is a schematic display anchor and is labelled as such in the data. It must not be interpreted as a surveyed substation location.

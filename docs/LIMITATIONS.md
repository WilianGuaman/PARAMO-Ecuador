# Interpretation

Results are planning-model outputs, not forecasts of actual dispatch. Monthly resolution represents seasonal operation; hourly flexibility and real-time security are outside this representation. The network is a reduced electrical model, and mapped corridors do not specify physical construction routes.

Adverse contains five realizations per pathway. Its empirical ranges should be read with that sample size in mind. A selected realization need not rank like its ensemble mean in ENS or emissions.

The independent six-node study uses its own assumptions and scenario definitions.

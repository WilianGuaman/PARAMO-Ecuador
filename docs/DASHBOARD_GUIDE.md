# Explorer guide

Select the model, pathway and hydrological case. Use the year slider to inspect annual states and the month selector for individual periods. Wet and Dry checkboxes filter monthly plots without changing whole-horizon indicators.

Transmission provides a separate selected-plan/ensemble control. The two maps share scales, and clicking a corridor selects its history. The annual additions heatmap separates the three investment alternatives.

Reservoir views compare storage cycles, operation and plant-level balances. The All figures tab provides the full figure collection. Charts retain zoom, hover, image export, presentation mode and shareable view links.

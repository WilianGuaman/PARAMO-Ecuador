# Dashboard Guide

The PARAMO Ecuador Results Explorer provides a common interface for two Ecuador study cases.

## Study case

Use **Study case** to switch between the Ecuador 24-Bus National Planning Case and the Ecuador 6-Bus Reduced Planning Case. Scenario and hydrology controls adapt to the selected case.

## National case filters

- Policy pathway: REF, BRIDGE, NZT.
- Hydrology: Baseline, Adverse.
- Statistic: Mean, P10, P50, P90, Representative realization.
- Year: 2025–2050.
- Month: January–December where monthly results are available.

## Dashboard sections

- Overview
- Generation Expansion
- Transmission & Reconductoring
- Hydrology & Reservoirs
- System Operation
- Emissions & Decarbonization
- Reliability & ENS
- Costs & Uncertainty
- Scenario Comparison
- Data & Downloads
- Methodology

## Download controls

Chart toolbars export the displayed data or figure. **Download selection** creates a ZIP containing the data associated with the active case, scenario and hydrology. The complete public dataset is available from the Data & Downloads section.

## Shareable views

The explorer encodes the active case, scenario, hydrology, statistic, year, month and section in the URL. Copying the view link reproduces the selected configuration.

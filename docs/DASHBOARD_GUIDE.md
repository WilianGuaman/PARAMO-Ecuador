# Explorer guide

Select the model, pathway and hydrological case. Use the year slider to inspect annual states and the month selector for individual periods. Wet and Dry checkboxes filter monthly plots without changing whole-horizon indicators.

Transmission provides a separate selected-plan/ensemble control. The two maps share scales, and clicking a corridor selects its history. Annual addition bars and cumulative 2025/2050 comparisons separate the three investment alternatives in the selected realizations.

Reservoir views compare storage cycles, operation and plant-level balances. Pathway scores compare six indicators using equal weights for the three dimensions. Charts retain zoom, hover, image export, presentation mode and shareable view links.

Year and month sliders cover 2025–2050 and January–December.

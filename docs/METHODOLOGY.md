# Methods and units

Annual investment decisions are evaluated with monthly operation over 2025–2050. Each model month represents 720 hours. Energy is shown in GWh, capacity in MW, direct emissions in ktCO₂ or MtCO₂ as labelled, discounted costs in MUSD and water volumes in hm³.

ENS is the sum of nodal power deficits multiplied by the system power base and monthly hours, divided by 1,000 to obtain GWh. Monthly, annual and cumulative views use the same records.

Baseline contains 100 realizations per pathway; Adverse contains five. Baseline representatives are closest to the mean total cost within the modal interval. Adverse representatives are closest to the median total cost. Ensemble means use the model weights; empirical P10–P90 ranges describe these samples and are not confidence intervals.

Wet and Dry labels follow each campaign’s input calendar. The campaigns also differ in operating assumptions, so their contrast is not an isolated hydrological effect.

Transmission maps distinguish reconductoring of existing circuits, new conventional circuits and new reinforced circuits. Selected-plan views show individual decisions; ensemble views report adoption shares and expected circuit counts. Annual heatmaps show increments, whereas maps show cumulative states.

Reservoir states are linked within each model year. January starts from its specified volume, and end-of-January and end-of-December volumes are constrained to match. Cascade transfers can reuse water downstream.

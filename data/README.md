# Public results

Chart-ready series are embedded in `assets/js/dashboard_data.js`. They contain aggregate indicators, empirical summaries and selected generation, capacity and transmission trajectories. Aggregate reservoir storage is reported without plant-level water balances. Annual demand totals support the ENS ratios. Geographic layers locate the displayed networks and public infrastructure.

Optimization code, model input databases, line electrical and unit-cost parameters, plant hydraulic parameters, sampled availability inputs and complete realization archives are excluded. Data exported from a chart remain public and reusable under the stated data license.
